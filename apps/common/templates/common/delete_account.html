Delete your Carryoo account

App: Carryoo (package com.carryoo.customer)

You can request deletion of your Carryoo customer account and associated personal data. You do not need to keep the app installed to submit a request.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to support@nashikflow.app from the email address linked to your account, if available.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Carryoo account.
  3. Include your registered phone number in international format (for example, +91XXXXXXXXXX) so we can verify your account.

What we delete

  • Account profile (name, phone number, and email)
  • Saved addresses
  • Booking history linked to your account
  • Authentication credentials for your Carryoo account

Processing time

Verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days. We may contact you if we need additional information to verify ownership of the account.

Data we may retain

We may retain limited records where required by law or for legitimate business purposes, such as payment or transaction records required for accounting, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Any retained data is kept only for the period required by applicable regulations.

Questions? Contact support@nashikflow.app.
